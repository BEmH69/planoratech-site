Why Pip's sheets look the way they do

1

Large print

Big, clear letters and numbers that are easy to read.

2

Wide spacing

Short lines and room between them, so the page never feels crowded.

3

One idea per page

Each sheet teaches one thing, so a child can finish and feel proud.

4

A color for each job

Instructions, examples, your turn and tips each have their own color.

Meet Pip

Pip is a friendly fox who cheers the child on from the first page to the star chart. Every pack has the same calm layout, so kids know what to expect.

Friendly design to make practice easier. These are practice sheets, not a medical or therapy product.
