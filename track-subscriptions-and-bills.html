How to track your subscriptions and bills in one place

Published 2026-10-25

Small recurring payments add up quietly. A single list of everything you pay regularly, with the due dates, usually shows savings you did not know you had.

Find everything you pay for

Go through three months of bank and card statements and highlight every recurring payment: streaming, apps, cloud storage, gym, insurance, phone, memberships. Do not forget yearly charges.

Build the list

One row per bill: name, amount, how often, due date, payment method and cancellation notice. Convert yearly amounts to a monthly figure so you can compare everything on the same scale.

See the total

Add up the monthly column and the yearly total. Most people are surprised: a few small subscriptions can cost several hundred euros a year.

Decide what stays

Mark each item as keep, review or cancel. Ask whether you used it in the last month. If not, cancel it, and note the date so you can check the charge actually stopped.

Set a monthly reminder

Put a repeating reminder in your calendar to open the list once a month. Five minutes is enough to catch price increases, trial periods that turned into paid plans and bills due this week.
