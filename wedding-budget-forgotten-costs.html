Wedding budget: the costs couples forget

Published 2026-10-25

Most couples budget for the venue, the food and the dress, then get surprised by everything else. A short list of the forgotten costs, and a plan for the payments, keeps the total under control.

Start with the total and the priorities

Decide the maximum you will spend and who contributes. Then pick the two or three things that matter most to you and protect those first.

Costs that are often missed

Alterations and accessories, tips for staff, a marriage license and administrative fees, hair and makeup trials, transport for guests, extra hours at the venue, decorations and late-night snacks.

Add a buffer

Reserve 5 to 10 percent of the total for the unexpected. Prices rise, guest numbers change and small extras appear in the last weeks.

Track deposits and due dates

For every supplier, note the total price, the deposit paid, the balance and the due date. Seeing what is paid and what is still owed avoids last-minute cash shocks.

Review the budget every month

Compare planned and actual each month. If one category goes over, decide which other one gives. Staying in control is easier when you look often and early.
