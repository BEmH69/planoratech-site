Debt snowball vs avalanche: which method should you choose?

Published 2026-10-25

Both methods pay your debts off faster than paying a little on everything. They only differ in which debt you attack first. The best method is the one you will actually keep doing.

The snowball method

List your debts from the smallest balance to the largest. Pay the minimum on all of them, and put every extra euro on the smallest. When it is gone, roll its payment onto the next one. The quick wins keep you motivated.

The avalanche method

List your debts from the highest interest rate to the lowest and put the extra money on the most expensive debt first. This costs less interest overall, but the first win can take longer to arrive.

How to choose

If you need motivation, pick the snowball. If you are disciplined and the rates are very different, the avalanche saves more money. Some people start with a snowball for one quick win, then switch to avalanche.

What to put in your sheet

For each debt: name, balance, interest rate, minimum payment. Add a total monthly amount you can commit, then a column that shows the balance after each payment. A countdown to the payoff date is a strong motivator.

Protect the plan

Keep a small buffer so an unexpected cost does not push you back onto a card. Review the plan every month and celebrate each debt you close.
