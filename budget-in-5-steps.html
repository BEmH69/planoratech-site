How to build a monthly budget in 5 steps

A budget does not need to be complicated. Here is a method that takes about 20 minutes the first time and 5 minutes a week after that.

1. Write down your income

List what you really receive each month after tax. If your income changes, use your lowest recent month.

2. List your fixed costs

Rent, insurance, subscriptions, loan payments. These barely change, so they come first.

3. Estimate your flexible spending

Groceries, transport, leisure, eating out. Look at last month's bank statement and round each category.

4. Pay yourself first

Decide a savings amount and treat it like a bill. Even a small amount builds the habit.

5. Compare planned and actual

Once a week, note what you spent and compare it with the plan. Adjust next month, not in a panic.
