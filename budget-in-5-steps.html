How to build a monthly budget in 5 steps

Published 2026-10-25

A budget does not need to be complicated. Here is a method that takes about 20 minutes the first time and 5 minutes a week after that. It works in Excel, Google Sheets or on paper.

1. Write down your income

List what you really receive each month after tax. If your income changes from month to month, use your lowest recent month. A budget built on the low number never lets you down.

2. List your fixed costs

Rent, insurance, subscriptions, loan payments. These barely change, so they come first. Add the due date next to each one, because timing matters as much as the amount.

3. Estimate your flexible spending

Groceries, transport, leisure, eating out. Look at last month's bank statement and round each category. Do not aim for perfect: a realistic number you can keep beats a strict one you abandon.

4. Pay yourself first

Decide a savings amount and treat it like a bill. Even a small amount builds the habit, and you can raise it later when your fixed costs go down.

5. Compare planned and actual

Once a week, note what you spent and compare it with the plan. Adjust next month, not in a panic. The comparison is where a budget turns into a habit.
