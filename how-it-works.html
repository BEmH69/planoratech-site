How it works

Everything is a digital download. You buy on Etsy, download your files right away, and nothing is shipped.

Choose

Pick the planner that fits what you want to follow, from the planners page.

Questions

About the planners

Which programs do I need for the planners?

Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. The files are .xlsx spreadsheets.

Which languages are in the planners?

Every planner includes English, French, German and Spanish. You choose from one dropdown.

About the learning sheets

How do I print the learning packs?

English packs are made for US Letter paper: print the PDF at 100 percent, or choose Fit to page on A4. Use the black and white file to save ink.

Which grade are the packs for?

Each pack is made for one grade only, written on the cover and in the title, so there is no mix of levels.

Are there packs in other languages?

Yes. Every language has its own packs, written for that country's school grades: Grade in English, CP to CM2 in French, Klasse 1 to 4 in German, Primaria in Spanish.

Are the learning sheets a treatment for dyslexia or dyscalculia?

No. They are practice sheets with a layout designed to be easy on the eyes. They are not a medical or therapy product.

About buying

Is it a physical product?

No. Everything is a digital download. You get your files right after purchase and nothing is shipped.

Where do I buy?

On the PlanoraTech Etsy shop. Every product card on this site links to its listing.
