Spreadsheet templates that stay simple

PlanoraTech designs Excel and Google Sheets templates for personal finance and small business: a monthly budget tracker, savings goals, bill and subscription tracking, debt payoff plans, a net worth tracker, an Etsy profit calculator, inventory and income tracking, a wedding budget planner and a habit tracker.

Each template is a single .xlsx file with English, French, German and Spanish built in. You pick your language from one dropdown, enter your numbers and the charts and totals update by themselves. It is an instant digital download, so you can start in minutes. Nothing is shipped.
