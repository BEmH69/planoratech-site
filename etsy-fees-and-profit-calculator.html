Etsy fees explained: how to calculate your real profit

Published 2026-10-25

Many new Etsy sellers look at the sale price and forget everything that is taken out of it. Knowing your real profit per sale is the difference between a hobby that costs money and a shop that grows.

The main costs to count

Etsy charges a small listing fee for each listing, a transaction fee on the sale price (shipping included) and a payment processing fee that depends on your country. Rates change, so check Etsy's current fee page before you price.

Do not forget your own costs

Add anything you pay to make the product: materials, printing, software, transparent packaging, advertising. For digital products the cost per sale is low, but your time and any subscriptions still count.

The formula

Profit per sale = sale price minus listing fee share, minus transaction fee, minus payment fee, minus your cost per item. Divide by the sale price to get your margin in percent.

Set a price that works

Start from the profit you want per sale and work backwards. If the margin looks too thin, raise the price a little or bundle products: a bundle raises the order value while the fixed fees stay the same.

Track it over the month

Keep one row per sale and one per expense, then look at the monthly total. Seeing fees, advertising and profit side by side tells you quickly whether to push a product or drop it.
