Emergency fund: how much should you save?

Published 2026-10-25

An emergency fund is money set aside for the unexpected: a repair, a job gap, a medical bill. It stops a bad month from becoming debt. The right amount depends on your life, but there is a simple way to find it.

Start with one month of essentials

Add up what you must pay each month: housing, food, utilities, insurance, transport, minimum debt payments. That is your essential monthly cost, not your full budget.

Choose your target

A common guideline is three to six months of essential costs. Three months can be enough with a stable job and no dependents. Aim closer to six if your income varies, you are self-employed or you support a family.

Begin with a small first goal

A first milestone of 500 or 1,000 euros already covers many small emergencies. Reaching it quickly proves the habit works and makes the larger target feel possible.

Automate the saving

Set a fixed transfer on payday, even a modest one. Money you never see is money you do not spend. Raise the amount whenever a bill disappears.

Keep it safe and separate

Use a separate savings account that is easy to reach but not linked to your daily card. When you use the fund, rebuild it before you start other goals.
