Planners (Excel and Google Sheets)

1

Choose

Pick the planner that fits what you want to follow, from the planners page.

2

Buy and download

Buy on Etsy, then download the .xlsx file from your Etsy purchases page. It is available right away.

3

Open

Open it in Excel, or upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. Pick your language on the Start Here tab.

4

Fill in

Type your numbers in the colored cells. Totals and charts update by themselves, no formulas to write.
